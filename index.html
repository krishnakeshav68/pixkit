Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded to PixKit?

PixKit is designed to process selected files in your browser. Files are not sent to a PixKit server for processing.

Can I convert multiple images to one PDF?

Yes. The Image → PDF tool lets you select multiple images and arrange them before creating the PDF.

Can PixKit compress images?

Yes. Compress Image supports presets and custom pixel and file-size limits, with the result size and percentage reduction shown before saving.

Can PixKit compress PDFs?

Yes. Compress PDF can reduce image-heavy PDFs in your browser. The compressed PDF may contain image-based pages, so selectable text may be lost.
